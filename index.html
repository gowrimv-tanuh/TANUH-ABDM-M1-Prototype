SAMVIT · TANUH

Workflow 1 — New patient registration, creating ABHA number/address

Pre-requisites

  1. Patient has no ABHA number/address
  2. Patient has Aadhaar-linked mobile
  3. Both FLW and patient mobile are online
  4. FLW is mapped to a facility registered as an ABDM identifier

FLW explains the benefits of ABHA; patient agrees to ABHA creation and registration to TANUH. Confirm the patient has no existing ABHA first — some patients get one during a health camp without realising it.

Tap the highlighted actions to move through the flow — this is a working prototype, not a real ABDM connection.
